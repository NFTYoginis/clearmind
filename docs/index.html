Questions

Will it tell me what to do?

No — and that's the point. ClearMind doesn't make decisions for you. It helps you understand what decision you're actually making, so the move you land on is genuinely yours.

Do you store my conversation?

No account, and the conversation isn't saved on our side. The only thing you keep is the crossing document — and only if you choose to save or print it.

Is this therapy?

No. It's a thinking companion for something specific that's weighing on you. It isn't a substitute for therapy or a crisis line — and if something heavier surfaces, it will point you to real help rather than try to hold it.

How long does it take?

As long as you need — usually a single sitting. Some people cross in a few minutes; others sit longer in the fog. There's no timer and no right pace.
